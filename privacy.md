# New File Here Privacy Policy

New File Here (the macOS app by Kevin Brown) does not collect, transmit or share any personal
data.

- The app runs entirely on your Mac. It has no network access and contacts no server.
- It creates empty documents only in folders you choose, when you ask it to from Finder's
  right-click menu or its menu bar icon.
- It keeps three things, only on your Mac, in the app's sandbox container: the folders you
  granted it access to (as macOS bookmarks), the last few folders you opened in Finder (so
  the menu bar icon can offer them), and whether it opens at login.
- There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

To remove everything it kept, quit the app and delete
`~/Library/Containers/com.kevinbrowncodes.FinderNewFile`.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/kevinbrowncodes/new-file-here-support/issues.
